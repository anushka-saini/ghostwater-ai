# GhostWater AI

Sparse-sensor water leak intelligence and maintenance triage.

**Detect. Localize. Prioritize. Explain.**

A decision-support system that finds leak-related anomalies in water distribution
networks, estimates their likely zone and impact, and ranks inspections and repairs,
even when pressure sensors are limited.

## Status

Work in progress. Benchmark: BattLeDIM / L-Town (simulated network).

## Important limits

- Results are on a simulated benchmark network, not a live city system.
- Demos are replays of historical benchmark data.
- Water savings are estimates under stated assumptions, not measured outcomes.
